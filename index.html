HOW TO PLAY

Tactical operations and survival guide

Objective & Lives

Fresh 3 Lives & 100% Health are granted at the start of every single level — no life loss carries over between sectors! Level 1 requires collecting all coins to unlock the portal. In Levels 2 through 15, coin collection is optional bonus score, and the exit portal is active immediately.

Controls

Arrows / WASD / HomeEndPgUpPgDn / Numpad — Move SPACE — Cyber Strike (Kill up to 3 enemies in sectors with 3+ sentinels) H — Navigation Hint (5 Hints Per Level) B — Tactical Powers Arsenal (Levels 8–15) P / ESC — Pause Game

Enemies & Combat

  • Enemy Elimination (3+ Enemies Sectors) — In sectors with 3 or more sentinels (Levels 8–15), you can eliminate enemies 2 to 3 times per sector on your way to the exit portal! Destroy them using Cyber Strike (SPACE), by running into frozen enemies (Cryo Shatter), via Aegis Shield deflection, or Safe Zone defense. Defeated hostiles respawn back at their spawn points in 3 seconds.
  • Collision Damage — Touching an active pink enemy inflicts 25 damage. If health drops to zero, 1 Life is lost. When a life is lost, health restores to 100%.
  • Max 4 Pink Enemies — Difficulty scales progressively from simpler to harder, strictly capped at a maximum of 4 pink enemies in any sector.
  • Safe Zones (Levels 11–15) — Advanced sectors feature glowing cyan Safe Zones. Enemies cannot enter or attack you while inside!
  • 5 Total Hints — Every sector provides 5 navigation hints to highlight the optimal corridor path to the exit.

Tactical Cyber Arsenal (Levels 13, 14 & 15)

In high-tier sectors, open the Tactical Arsenal (B or POWERS button) to equip defensive protection using your accumulated score:

  • 🛡️ Aegis Forcefield (80 Pts) — Absorbs the next enemy strike completely and deflects the attacker with zero damage.
  • ❄️ Chrono Cryo Stasis (140 Pts) — Freezes all hostile pink sentinels for 20 seconds.
  • ⚙️ Nanite Composite Armor (200 Pts) — Hexagonal cyber-shield cuts collision damage by 50% (only 12 HP lost) and grants 2.5x longer invulnerability.
  • 💠 Portable Sanctuary Bubble (280 Pts) — Creates a mobile Safe Zone around you for 15 seconds that repels enemies everywhere.

Collectible Power-Ups

  • Speed Overdrive — Boosts movement velocity by 60%.
  • Cyber Shield — Absorbs one lethal enemy collision safely.
  • Cryo Freeze — Freezes all enemy sentinels in place for 20 seconds.
  • Bonus Gems & Cores — Grant substantial score bonuses (+50 to +200) to invest in tactical defenses.

Sector Milestone Completion Bonuses

  • Level 1 Completion — +500 Bonus Points awarded upon clearing.
  • Level 5 Completion — +1,000 Bonus Points awarded upon clearing.
  • Level 10 Completion — +1,500 Bonus Points awarded upon clearing.
  • Level 15 Completion — +2,000 Bonus Points awarded upon final victory!
  • In-Between Levels — Standard clearing with 0 bonus points (pure skill & collectible score).

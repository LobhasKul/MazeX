HOW TO PLAY

Tactical operations and survival guide

Objective

Level 1 requires collecting all coins to unlock the exit portal. In Levels 2 through 15, coin collection is optional bonus score, and the exit portal is open from the start.

Controls

WASD / Arrows — Move H — Hint Path P / ESC — Pause Game

Enemies & Combat

  • Enemy Collision — Touching an active pink enemy inflicts 25 damage. If health drops to zero, 1 Life is lost. If all lives are lost, the mission ends.
  • Random Cyber Operatives — Each deployment assigns a random cyber operative with unique aesthetic armor, animations, and radar signatures.
  • AI Personalities — Blinky chases directly, Pinky ambushes ahead, Inky patrols fast, and Clyde guards key paths.

Power-Ups

  • Speed Overdrive — Boosts movement velocity by 60%.
  • Cyber Shield — Absorbs one lethal enemy collision safely.
  • Cryo Freeze — Freezes all enemy sentinels in place.

Tactical Tips

  • The exit portal stays dormant until all required coins and cores are collected.
  • Use hints sparingly to calculate the shortest path to objectives.
  • Watch enemy gaze direction to predict incoming ambushes.
